When you look up at the sky at night, what do you see?  If it's not cloudy you may see lots of glittering stars!  But, remember that the sun is also a star.  The stars in the night sky do not look like the sun. They do not look as big or as bright. But they are. The stars in the night sky are made of hot gas, just like the sun!   If we could get close to the stars, they would look bigger, brighter, and more like the sun.  But the stars we see at night are so far away that no one from Earth has ever been able to get close to them.


Scientists who study the stars and outer space are called astronomers.  Astronomers have discovered that stars differ in many ways.  Stars can be different sizes and colors.  Some stars are closer to Earth than others and some stars are hotter than others. Astronomers also discovered that stars cluster together in large groups.  A large group of stars that cluster together in one area is called a galaxy. The galaxy to which our sun and solar system belong is called the Milky Way Galaxy. 

Scientists think there are billions of galaxies in the universe.  There are billions of stars in each galaxy and billions of galaxies in the universe. That is almost more than you can think about !
